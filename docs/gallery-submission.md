# Comfy Gallery submission concept

## Working title

One Character, Three Worlds — A MiniMax H3 Reference Workflow

## Creative brief

Create one polished 20–30 second piece in ComfyUI. Preserve one lead character across three distinct environments. Use visible continuity, deliberate camera movement, and an authored soundscape. The work should stand on its own and must not be a product advertisement.

## Suggested sequence

1. Rainy neon street — low-angle tracking shot.
2. Quiet sunlit workshop — slow push-in with identity preserved.
3. Windy coastal cliff — wide orbit with continuous clothing and facial details.
